About Me

I am an Integrated PhD scholar at the Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI), Prayagraj, working in High Energy Physics.

This site hosts my academic notes, solution manuals, and occasional writing.

Research

  • Scalar Catalyzed Nuclear Fusion — Deriving mass limits for a hypothetical scalar fusion catalyst particle, building on Jackson (1957). Analysing lower bounds via WKB barrier penetration and upper bounds via nuclear absorption processes.

Research interests: HEP Phenomenology, Quantum Field Theory.
